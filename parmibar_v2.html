Provenance & Story

From the hills of Parma
to your table

Parmigiano Reggiano is one of the world's oldest and most respected foods. For over 800 years it has been produced in exactly the same way, in exactly the same region of northern Italy — no exceptions, no shortcuts.

PARMIBAR was born from a simple conviction: this extraordinary cheese deserves better than a supermarket shelf. We work directly with family-owned caseifici in Parma, selecting wheels aged to precise maturity, hand-broken using the traditional knife method that respects the crystalline structure nature built.

  • Only 300 authorized dairies in the world are permitted to produce authentic Parmigiano Reggiano DOP
  • Every wheel must be inspected and stamped by the Consorzio del Parmigiano Reggiano before release
  • Minimum 12 months aging — our range starts at 24 months for full crystalline development
  • Naturally lactose-free after 18 months of aging — the fermentation process eliminates it completely
  • The highest natural protein density of any aged cheese: over 35g protein per 100g

Why Parmigiano Reggiano

The undisputed
King of Cheeses

Eight centuries of mastery. A single protected region. Strict production laws that have never changed. There is no substitute.

01

Protected Origin

Authentic Parmigiano Reggiano can only be produced in the provinces of Parma, Reggio Emilia, Modena, Bologna, and Mantua. The EU's DOP designation makes this legally binding — anything produced elsewhere cannot carry the name.

02

The Crystalline Structure

Those white flecks inside every aged piece are tyrosine crystals — amino acids that form only after years of patient aging. They are a natural quality signal: more crystals, deeper flavor. They cannot be manufactured or accelerated.

03

Natural Protein Density

Parmigiano Reggiano contains more protein per gram than almost any other natural food — over 35g per 100g. It is also naturally lactose-free after 18 months of aging, making it digestible for the majority of those who struggle with dairy.

04

No Additives, Ever

By law, authentic Parmigiano Reggiano contains three ingredients: raw milk, salt, and rennet. No colorings, no preservatives, no stabilizers. The DOP specification is absolute and monitored by the Consorzio on every wheel produced.

05

Artisan Breaking

Parmigiano is never cut — it is broken. The traditional almond-shaped knife follows the natural grain of the cheese, preserving the crystalline texture that makes each piece irregular, alive, and unmistakably real. Every PARMIBAR portion is broken by hand at source.

06

800 Years Unchanged

The monks of the Po Valley first perfected this cheese in the twelfth century. The method — the milk, the copper vats, the morning production, the patient aging — has not materially changed since. Time is the ingredient no other cheese can buy.
